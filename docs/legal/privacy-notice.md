## What BridgeTogether is
An educational tool that helps partners reflect privately and choose what to share. It is not
therapy, counselling, diagnosis, a crisis service or an emergency service.

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Legal basis (to confirm) |
|------|-----|--------------------------|
| Email address and password (stored as a salted hash), sign-in and security settings | To run your account and keep it secure | Contract |
| What you write: reflections, topics, exercise answers, drafts | To let you use the private features | Contract; explicit consent for special-category data you choose to write |
| Messages you choose to share, and a record of your approval | To deliver them to your partner and prove what you agreed to send | Contract |
| Your safety check answers and safety mode | To hold shared features and show support options | Explicit consent; you can delete them at any time |
| Pace settings and in-app notices (no message text) | To respect your quiet hours and pauses | Contract |
| Pilot enrollment (which documents you agreed to, when) | To show you agreed to take part | Consent |
| Feedback you send (linked to you only if you ask us to contact you) | To improve the pilot | Legitimate interests |
| Security records (ids and times only, never what you wrote) | To protect accounts and investigate misuse | Legitimate interests |
| Counts of activity (for example "messages sent today"), never linked to a person | To run and size the service | Legitimate interests |

What you write may reveal information about health, sex life or other sensitive matters. We
only process it to provide the features you use, and we never use it for advertising,
profiling or training AI models.

## Who can see it
- **You.** Everything private is visible only to you.
- **Your partner** sees only messages you preview and approve, and only while you are both in the
  workspace. They never see your private writing, your safety answers or whether you have any.
- **The AI writing coach**, if switched on, receives the text you ask it to help with, sent to
  **[AI provider]** under a data processing agreement that forbids keeping it or training on it.
  Until that agreement is in place the coach does not send text anywhere.
- **Our hosting provider [hosting provider]** stores the data, encrypted, in **[region]**.
- **Our team** does not read your private writing. Operators can access the database only to run
  the service, with access logged.
- We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

## How long we keep it
Until you delete it or your account, except: discarded drafts (30 days), in-app notices (90
days), coaching usage counts (90 days), feedback (1 year), security records (400 days, ids only),
backups (14 days). Full details are in the data inventory, available on request.

## Your rights
You can download all your data or delete your account at any time from **Account › Personal
data**; deletion takes effect immediately and backups age out within 14 days. You can withdraw
from the pilot at any time on the **Pilot** page. You can also ask us to correct or restrict your
data, or object to processing, by writing to **[privacy contact email]**, and you may complain to
**[supervisory authority]**.

## Security
Encryption in transit and at rest, strict per-person access in the code, no private text in
logs, and regular security testing. No system is perfectly secure; if someone else may access
your device, use "Leave quickly" and sign out.

## Changes
If we change this notice we will ask you to read and agree to the new version before you
continue using the pilot.
