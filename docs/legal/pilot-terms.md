## About the pilot

## What BridgeTogether is, and isn't
BridgeTogether is an educational communication tool. **It is not therapy, counselling,
diagnosis, mediation, a crisis service or an emergency service**, and no clinician is watching
what you write. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency number. The
Support page lists other services.

Nothing in the app tells you to stay in, repair or leave your relationship. You never have to
share anything, reply to anything or use the app with your partner.

## Your part
- Be 18 or over and use your own account.
- Don't use the app to threaten, harass, monitor or control anyone.
- Don't try to access anyone else's data or break the app's security.
- Tell us about problems, including anything that felt unsafe, using the feedback form.

## Early software
The pilot may have bugs, change, or be unavailable. We may switch features off at short notice
to protect people. Please don't rely on it as your only way to communicate something important.

## Your data
How we handle your data is in the [privacy notice](/privacy-notice). You can download or delete
your data at any time.

## Leaving
You can withdraw at any time on the Pilot page, with no reason needed. You can then keep your
data, download it, or delete your account. We may end your participation if these terms are
broken or to keep someone safe.
