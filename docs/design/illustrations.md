# Illustrations

## Landing page hero: many kinds of couples

- File: `src/Couples.Web/wwwroot/images/couples-hero.svg`
- Shown on: the home page (`Components/Pages/Home.razor`)
- Source: original artwork drawn for BridgeTogether in this repository. It is
  generated by `tools/illustrations/couples_hero.py` (plain Python, no
  dependencies), so it can be changed and regenerated.
- License: owned by the project. No third-party art, stock images, or fonts
  are embedded, so there is no attribution requirement and no restriction on
  commercial use.
- Palette: the Warm theme colours from `Components/Layout/AppTheme.cs`
  (terracotta `#A94A33`, sage `#4F6B5A`, cream background `#FAF5EE`) plus a few
  supporting tones.

What it shows (also the image's alt text): six couples holding hands, chosen
so that many kinds of people can see themselves in the product:

1. An older Black couple (grey hair, glasses).
2. Two women, one plus-size.
3. A woman in a hijab and her partner.
4. A man using a wheelchair and his partner.
5. Two men, one older.
6. A plus-size couple.

To change it, edit the script, then run:

```
python3 tools/illustrations/couples_hero.py src/Couples.Web/wwwroot/images/couples-hero.svg
```

Keep the alt text in `Home.razor` in step with what the picture shows.
