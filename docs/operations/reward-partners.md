# Recruiting date-night partners

A starting playbook for signing up local businesses. The software side is in ADR-0018.

## Who to approach
Start with **one town**. Ten partners in one place make the rewards page useful; one each in ten
places doesn't. Good first partners are independent and decided by the owner on the spot:
restaurants, cafés, dessert bars, cinemas, bowling, mini golf, paint or pottery studios, escape
rooms, bookshops with a café. Chains rarely decide locally.

## What to ask for
Something small that brings in a paying table of two, not a free meal:
- a dessert or starter to share with two mains
- two-for-one on a quiet night (Monday to Wednesday)
- 15–20% off for two, or a free drink each
Ask them to pick a monthly cap (for example 10 vouchers) so the cost is known in advance.

## The pitch (email or in person)
> Hi [name], I run BridgeTogether, an app that helps couples communicate better. When a couple
> has a good few weeks, we reward them with a date night at a local place. I'd like [business]
> to be one of the places we send them to. It's free: you choose a small offer (say, a dessert
> to share with two mains on weeknights) and a monthly limit. Couples arrive with a one-time
> code that you check on a private web page. Could I pop in for five minutes this week?

Then send them to `/partners` to apply, or fill the form in with them.

## Where to find them
- Walk in mid-afternoon on a weekday and ask for the owner or manager.
- The Chamber of Commerce and the downtown or main-street business association.
- Local business Facebook groups and the regional restaurant association.

## The agreement (one page)
Have a lawyer review it before use. It should say:
- the exact offer, days it applies and the monthly cap
- vouchers are single-use, how long they are valid, and that the business marks them used
- no payment either way; either side can end it with notice (for example 14 days), and vouchers
  already issued are still honoured until they expire
- how the business may be named on the site
Gift certificate and coupon rules (expiry limits, disclosures) vary by state.

## After they say yes
1. Approve the application on `/admin/partners` with the agreed title and a credit cost.
2. Send them the check link shown once on that page. Make a new one if it is lost or shared.
3. Check in after the first month with how many couples visited (the page shows issued and used counts).

## Later
Keep it free until you can show numbers for their area. Paid or featured placement is easier to
sell with "N couples near you earned a date night last month".
